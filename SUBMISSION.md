### c. Project Description
```text
Project Name: HORIZON — Reverse-Engineered Countdown Lock Screen
Mission: Handshake AI Skills Studio × Google "Your Deadline, Your Design"
Target Focus: 30-Day Tech Career Offer & Capstone Sprint

1. The Problem & The Core Concept:
Traditional countdown timers display dwindling hours, which often induces anxiety rather than focused action. A countdown without direction creates paralysis. Built using Google Gemini's reverse-engineering methodology, Horizon flips passive countdowns into an active, glanceable Heads-Up Display (HUD) for mobile and desktop lock screens.

2. Gemini Reverse-Engineered Milestone Architecture:
Instead of fixating solely on the final December deadline, we used Google Gemini to work backward and decompose the 30-day journey into five sequential, actionable milestones:
- M-30 [Completed]: Handshake Profile & ATS Resume Overhaul
- M-21 [Active Focus]: 20 Tailored Applications & Portfolio V1 Shipped
- M-14 [Upcoming]: Technical Coding & Behavioral STAR Mock Interviews
- M-07 [Upcoming]: Final Round Onsite Interviews & Executive Panels
- M-00 [Victory Goal]: Offer Letter Negotiated, Accepted & Celebrated

3. Device Safe-Zone Engineering (Phone & Desktop):
A great lock screen must respect the operating system. Horizon is strictly calibrated for modern phone displays (9:19.5, 1179 x 2556) and computer screens (16:10, 2880 x 1800):
- Clock Safe Zone: The top 24% of the wallpaper features soft ambient dark space, ensuring 100% contrast and legibility for the native iOS / Android system clock, date, and notification widgets.
- Thumb & Dock Safe Zones: The bottom edge reserves clean space for flashlight, camera triggers, and home swipe bars.
- High-Information Hierarchy: Center cards feature days/hours/minutes remaining, a radial pacing ring (elapsed vs. remaining), and the single active milestone currently requiring focus.

4. Daily Accountability & Calendar Synchronization:
The average college student or professional unlocks their phone between 80 and 100 times per day. Horizon turns every single glance into a subconscious nudge keeping the user aligned with their current milestone. Furthermore, Horizon features an automated .ics calendar generator that syncs all reverse-engineered milestone dates and alarms directly into Google Calendar.
```
